# Proposal: Live deep research on the install, documented

Story: **US-16.7**, by [Decision 0034](../../../docs/engineering/decisions/0034-deep-research-like-content-writer.md) R1 and R2.

## Why

US-16.6 made a run like Deep-Research-Content-Writer work on fakes and disposable stacks. The owner asked to see it work for real on the research "langchain for deep agents", as an Expert and as a workflow, and then to have it documented in a README. They approved two live runs of up to US$5.00 each, started from the Studio.

## What Changes

- The owner updates the install: restart on the US-16.6 code, migration 64, and the concept seed, which adds the Deep research writer and publishes its workflow. Done by the owner on 2026-11-18.
- The coordinator starts, from the Studio, one Deep research writer run and one Deep research content writer execution on "langchain for deep agents", each with a US$5.00 budget. The owner decides the workflow's gate.
- `README-Deep-Research-Content-Writer.md` documents the reference, how Autobots runs it as an Expert and as a workflow, and what each run did as observed. `README.md` and `README-DEV.md` link to it, and `README.md`'s seed counts are corrected to seven Experts and seven workflows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `deep-research`: adds the live, documented runs on the install.

## Impact

No code changes. The owner's persistent install, its spend (at most US$10.00 across both runs), `README-Deep-Research-Content-Writer.md`, `README.md`, `README-DEV.md` and `CODEBASE.md`.
